# AI Study Pal (working title)

An AI study assistant that tests what a student already knows, teaches only what they're missing in a fun style they pick, and proves the learning worked with a before-and-after score.

## The Unique Kernel
It tests first, then teaches only the gaps. If the student already knows the parts of a foundation but not the materials, it skips the first and explains the second, in the style they chose (5-year-old, gossip, nerd), with simple visuals. A final test against the baseline shows whether the teaching worked.

## Who It's For
A student stuck on a unit topic: a building construction student trying to understand foundations, or a medical student trying to understand how cells work. High school, college and university students, and anyone with the passion to understand something.
Today they ask an AI for a well-labelled image, copy and paste it, and move on without understanding what each part does or what materials it uses, because they weren't asked to explain those.

## The Core Loop
They add a course and unit with its study materials, take a baseline mock test across the topics, see which topics they know and which they're weak in, pick a teaching style, get taught the weak topics, then take a final test and see their before-and-after result.
They come back because learning is fun in the style they pick, and because the result proves they actually understand more than before.

## Inspiration & Identity
Fun, friendly and playful in tone. Teaching styles such as "explain like I'm 5", gossip, nerd, and more. Convincing, fun messages that nudge students not to cheat. Badges for commitment and performance are part of the long-term vision. No reference apps or URLs given.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
Students rely on AI to give them answers without understanding them, and long PDFs are hard to digest. The learner wants students to learn their units in the most fun and productive way.

## What "Working" Looks Like
A new user opens the app and sees an empty screen with an add button. They enter a course and level, add a unit with its study material and the topic they're at, and the AI reads it. They take a baseline mock test across the topics and see their results (strong and weak topics). They pick a teaching style, the AI teaches the weak topics, and a final test shows the before-and-after score.
The "oh, that's cool" beat: the before-and-after score on a weak topic.

## The POC Boundary
- One unit with its study text or PDF
- A baseline mock test across the topics, with a result showing strong and weak topics
- A choice of teaching style (5-year-old, gossip, nerd)
- The AI teaches the weak topics in that style
- A final test with the before-and-after score

## Later
- Badges and commitment tracking: needs data across sessions, not needed to prove the kernel
- Multiple courses and units: the proof works with one
- Audio, video and image uploads: text/PDF is enough for the proof
- Blurred styles unlocked by mock-test performance: a reward layer on top of the core
- Learning-pattern detection (the AI learns which style works for the student): needs history across many sessions
- Exam mode for school exam revision: builds on the same tests
- "What's missing from this diagram" visual test: needs generated or classified images, bigger than the proof

## Explicitly Cut
Nothing is permanently cut. Everything deferred is listed under Later, each with its reason.
