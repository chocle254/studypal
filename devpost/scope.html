AI Study Pal (working title)

Tests what you know, teaches only what you're missing, and proves it worked with a before-and-after score.

Status: approved. Markdown (scope.md) is the canonical version.

The core loop

The unique kernel

It tests first, then teaches only the gaps. A student who knows the parts of a foundation but not the materials skips the first and learns the second, in the style they chose, with simple visuals. The final test against the baseline proves the teaching worked.

Who it's for

A student stuck on a unit topic: a building construction student on foundations, a medical student on how cells work. High school, college, university, or anyone with the passion to understand something.

Today: they ask an AI for a well-labelled image, copy and paste it, and move on without understanding what each part does or which materials it uses.

What "working" looks like

A new user sees an empty screen with an add button. They enter a course and level, add a unit with its material and the topic they're at, and the AI reads it. They take a baseline mock test, see strong and weak topics, pick a style, get taught the weak topics, and take a final test that shows their before-and-after score.

The POC boundary, and what waits

Now: in the proof of concept
  • One unit with its study text or PDF
  • Baseline mock test across the topics, with a strong/weak result
  • Teaching style choice: 5-year-old, gossip, nerd
  • The AI teaches the weak topics in that style
  • Final test with the before-and-after score
Later: worth doing, with reasons
  • Badges and commitment tracking: needs data across sessions, not needed to prove the kernel
  • Multiple courses and units: the proof works with one
  • Audio, video and image uploads: text/PDF is enough for the proof
  • Blurred styles unlocked by performance: a reward layer on top of the core
  • Learning-pattern detection: needs history across many sessions
  • Exam mode: builds on the same tests
  • "What's missing from this diagram" test: needs generated or classified images, bigger than the proof
Explicitly cut

Nothing is permanently cut. Everything deferred is listed under Later, each with its reason.

Feel

Fun, friendly and playful. Teaching styles such as "explain like I'm 5", gossip and nerd. Convincing, fun nudges not to cheat.
