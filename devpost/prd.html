What changed during the build

The plan below was approved before coding. Changes made while each slice was tried on a phone (full list in checklist.md → Revisions):

  • Rounds: about 15 pages per part, up to 5 topics, baseline → lessons → final test → before-and-after.
  • Final test unlocks only when every lesson check in the round is done.
  • Fix this topic for any topic under 70%: a second-pass lesson and a 5-question retake.
  • Ask about this on every lesson step; Go deeper at the end of a lesson.
  • Courses menu, profile, and a sample unit on the first screen. Name: Study Pal.

Scope said it. Now we know exactly what it means.

Scope said "a baseline mock test, teach the weak topics, a final test." Now it's five questions per topic with a progress box, a per-topic check after every lesson, and a final test on the same parts with different questions.

Acceptance criteria

Home and navigation
  • Empty state shows a plus button and "no units added yet".
  • Units show in two columns, each card with an image and name.
  • Burger opens a dropdown of added courses.
  • Pomodoro button starts a visible study-block timer.
  • Avatar opens profile details and overall performance across units.
Adding a unit
  • Plus asks for course and level, then moves to the add-unit screen.
  • The add-unit screen takes the unit, material and current topic.
Tests
  • Baseline: one topic, five questions, remaining topics shown unmarked.
  • Results show strong and weak topics.
  • Final test covers the same parts with different questions and structure.
  • Before and after scores show side by side.
Lessons
  • Step by step, easy English, many images, real-life analogies.
  • The chosen style changes the explanation.
  • A strong topic shows a nudge and a quick-check option.

States

First use, all-strong, between visits, sign-in
  • First use: empty home with plus button.
  • All topics strong: a nudge and a quick-check option.
  • Between visits: units and scores are still there; no sign-in. Storage is a 4-spec question.
  • Sign-in: not in the hackathon version; planned later.

Deferred, and open questions

Deferred from the POC
  • Badges, sign-in and accounts, multi-device sync, PC version
  • Audio, video and image uploads, unlockable styles
  • Learning-pattern detection, exam mode, diagram-based tests
Open questions
  • Where lesson images come from (before 4-spec)
